About

How I work

Ready to scale, reposition, or transform how marketing delivers growth? That’s what I do.

I’ve led change and delivered results for disruptive companies at all stages, in the US and around the world. I’ve built teams, launched and repositioned products and brands, scaled growth and improved how marketing operates. From beta tests and rollouts to boardrooms, I combine strategic perspective with hands-on execution as a pragmatic senior leader.

Curiosity drives my approach. I draw on research, analysis and experimentation to understand customers in depth, then turn those insights into clear decisions and action. I’m equally comfortable investing to accelerate growth or finding smarter, more efficient ways to operate, while staying accountable for unit economics and ROI. I partner across Marketing, Product, Sales, Engineering, Finance and Operations to put those decisions into practice.

I thrive on change. My career has been shaped by moments when technology changes markets: the web, mobile, social and now AI. I move between strategy and detailed execution, using AI and technology to help teams accomplish more in a flatter organization. I set a high bar and aim to lead with compassion. I’d be glad to discuss where I could make an impact in your organization.
